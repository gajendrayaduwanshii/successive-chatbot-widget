## Common Failure Patterns

1. **Company authority and page selection** — company facts sometimes retrieved BigCommerce, case-study, blog, press-release, or careers evidence instead of About/API sections.
2. **Structured ACF completeness** — core values, leadership lists, certifications, locations, tools, awards, and partner inventories were often omitted despite API fields or media labels.
3. **Career intent over-triggering** — words such as “application” or “testing” caused non-career business questions to route to live openings.
4. **Broad partner retrieval** — “any partnership” returned a false no-match, and partner-benefit questions mixed employee-perspective content with the canonical page.
5. **Technology/category mismatch** — frontend/backend/mobile and short tech questions sometimes returned immersive-tech, editorial, or awards content.
6. **Business-problem mapping** — modernization, slow development, automation, and global-partner needs produced jobs, Shopify-only, generic articles, or no-match responses.
7. **Culture/career evidence mixing** — learning, employee growth, inclusion, and benefits frequently retrieved service/blog evidence rather than Culture or Careers ACF sections.
8. **Freshness and specificity** — “latest awards” did not reliably enumerate the newest API-supported recognition with clear date evidence.
9. **Rate limiting/provider latency** — the unchanged run produced timeout/fetch failures and later 429 responses, preventing all planned conversation chains from yielding usable answers.
